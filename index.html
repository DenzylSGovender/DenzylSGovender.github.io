01 / ABOUT

From IT practitioner to education researcher.

I am a Computer Science Lecturer and researcher with a professional background spanning IT support, programming, academic leadership, curriculum development and higher education teaching.

My academic journey includes a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, a BSc Honours in Computing, a Master of Education and current doctoral studies in Educational Studies.

My current work focuses on how technology and artificial intelligence can shape teaching, learning and curriculum practices in STEM education, particularly within higher education.
